Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you use ShopEase and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, mobile number and delivery address. When you place an order, we record the order details needed to fulfil it (items, quantities, price, delivery address).

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, provide customer support, send order updates, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. We use secure sessions to protect your account.

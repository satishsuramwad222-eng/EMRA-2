Return Policy

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's how returns work.

Eligibility

Returns can be requested for delivered orders from the My Orders page. Select the order, choose "Request a Return", and tell us the reason.

Process

  1. Return Requested — we receive your request
  2. Return Approved — our team reviews and approves it
  3. Pickup Scheduled — a pickup is arranged
  4. Product Received — we receive the returned item
  5. Refund Processing — your refund is being processed
  6. Refund Completed — refund issued to your original payment method
